Freshly Picked Questions

Find answers to our top asked questions here, or send us a message if you can’t find what you’re looking for!

What pesticides are on your berries?

We adhere to the FDA’s rigorous standards on pesticide usage and residue while additionally utilizing natural and pesticide reducing methods of field management as much as possible.

Do you use Apeel or any preservatives on your berries?

No, we do not.

Are your berries GMO?

No, they are not. We do not sell any GMO berries.

Do you issue refunds?

No, we cannot issue refunds directly. The best strategy is to take the product back to the retailer you purchased them at with your receipt and ask for a refund.

Can I buy berries directly from Berry Fresh?

Unfortunately no, we do not sell direct-to-consumers and can only sell through grocery retailers.

Do you issue coupons?

We do not issue coupons but work directly with your local grocery retailers to implement promotion/discount periods as much as possible.
